# Daily affirmations: pool

366 affirmations, numbered. One was written for each row of the plan, so every date has an affirmation that is new that day. The topic it was written for is shown in italics.

## How they will be assigned (Phase 3)

- Each date gets three affirmations.
- The first is the one written for that date's topic. It appears as the lead on that date only.
- The other two are recycled from the rest of the pool, chosen from different subjects.
- No date repeats an affirmation within itself, no trio appears twice, and every affirmation is used the same number of times across the year: once as the day's own and twice as a recycled one.

## Note on first person

Affirmations are written as "I" because the reader says them about themselves. Everything else (readings, gratitude lines) addresses the reader as "you". Nothing here speaks as the site author.
