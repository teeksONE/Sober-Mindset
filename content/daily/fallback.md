# Fallback

Shown on any date whose own entry is not approved yet. It must make sense on every day of the year.

## fallback · Today
status: approved

Whatever day this is, it asks the same thing of you as every other. Stay with today. The year can wait, the past can wait, and nobody knows how the future turns out. Eat something decent. Drink some water. Move your body for a few minutes. Speak to one person honestly. If an urge comes, let it rise and pass without acting on it. If the day goes badly, you can still end it sober, and that counts in full. If it goes well, notice it. Tonight, when you get to bed, you will have added one more day to the life you are building. That is enough work for anyone.

**Gratitude:** Today I'm grateful for this day, exactly as it is, and for being here to live it.
