# Daily readings: plan

366 fixed dates, keyed MM-DD. One point per date. No readings are written yet.

## How the calendar is laid out

There are no monthly themes. People start on every day of the year, so every month carries a mix of everything: starting, cravings, people, body, feelings, and the rest. Entries were placed at random, with three rules applied to the shuffle:

- No two neighbouring dates share a subject.
- No month has more than four entries on one subject.
- Entries that sit close to each other in meaning (listed at the bottom) are at least three weeks apart.

The Subject column is only a tag for checking balance. It will not appear on the site.
