You're here if

  • You've got a week or more behind you
  • The evenings are long and the cravings arrive out of nowhere
  • You know you should "get support" and have no idea what that looks like
  • The idea of a church hall full of sixty-year-olds makes you want to drink

The job of this stage

Find at least one room, one person and one way through a craving. I got sober in AA and I'll always credit it. But I also know how daunting those rooms can be when everyone in them has decades on you. So this stage covers the rooms and everything beyond them. The research is clear that connection is the thing. Which kind is up to you.
