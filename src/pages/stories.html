Mine, in short

At 22 I couldn't do anything without a drink in my hand. I thought that was just what being young looked like, because everyone around me looked the same.

I got sober on November 12, 2014, at 29. The decade between 19 and 29 is the most expensive thing I own. I say that without self-pity. It's just the price, and I'd like you to have at 18 the information I had at 30.

What I didn't expect was what came next. Ski seasons. Skydiving. Hunting, travel, starting over more than once. Camping on a beach with the love of my life. The life I thought I was giving up turned out to be one I'd never had access to.

The Crossroads is the longer version of where it went wrong. A Life Worth Showing Up For is the longer version of where it went right.
