Before you start

First Summer is a list of ideas, not a supervised programme. Nobody from Sober Mindset runs, instructs or supervises any of it. You do it on your own, in your own time.

  • It's written for people 18 and over. If you're under 18, only do it with a parent or guardian involved.
  • These activities carry a real risk of injury. You take part at your own risk and you're responsible for your own safety.
  • Learn from a qualified instructor or an accredited school, club or operator. Don't teach yourself from videos.
  • Check your provider is insured, and follow their safety rules.
  • If you have an injury or a health condition, check with a doctor first.

Ideas for the Send

It should have a date, involve other people and be slightly out of reach today.

  • Surf: a dawn session at a new break, catching unbroken waves
  • Lifesaving: pass your Bronze Medallion and do a first patrol
  • Climbing: a grade two harder than you started on, or a first outdoor day with a guide
  • Combat: a first stripe, or an in-house beginners' competition
  • MTB: a clean run of a trail that beat you in week five
  • Trail: a 10 km trail race, or a parkrun personal best with your crew pacing
  • Skate: drop in on the big bowl
  • Sky: a tandem jump you paid for with what you didn't spend on nights out

When it gets hard

Around week six the novelty wears off, you're still a beginner and your mates are going out. That is the crossroads, in miniature. Go to training anyway. The people who are good at anything are just the ones who kept turning up after it stopped being new.

If drinking or drugs are pulling at you harder than you expected, that's worth taking seriously, not hiding. Stage 1 is a quiet place to start.
