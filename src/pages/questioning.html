You're here if

  • You've typed some version of "am I drinking too much" into a search bar
  • The morning-after promises to yourself keep getting broken by Friday
  • Someone you trust has said something, and it stung because it landed
  • You're 18 and nothing is wrong yet, but you can see where your crowd is heading

The job of this stage

Get honest information and look at it squarely. You don't need a label, a rock bottom or a plan. I spent years stuck here because I thought admitting a problem meant signing up for a smaller life. It doesn't, and the third page below is about exactly that.

Not yet

You don't have to tell anyone, pick a programme or say the word "forever". If the self-check worries you, the next stage covers how to stop safely. If it doesn't, you've lost ten minutes and gained a clear head.
