Why these four

HALT is old advice from the rooms: don't let yourself get too hungry, angry, lonely or tired. It has lasted because it's practical. Each of the four feels like a craving from the inside, and each has a fix that isn't a drink.

Do it at the same time every day. Tie it to something you already do, like the first coffee or brushing your teeth at night.
