The stages are a map, not a rulebook. People skip ahead, double back and do two at once. Start wherever today is.

Researchers have described change as a sequence of stages since the 1980s: not ready, thinking about it, preparing, acting, maintaining. People cycle through them more than once, and the kind of help that works depends on the stage. That is why this site is laid out as a path.
