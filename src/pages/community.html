Meetings are the most accessible free fellowship there is, and for a lot of us they were the way in. But rooms can be daunting when everyone in them has decades on you. Some people find their community on a mat, in the surf or at the bottom of a climbing wall first, and find the rooms later, or alongside. All of it counts.

When researchers looked at why meetings help people stay sober, the biggest factor was the change in who people spent their time with. A sober social circle is the active ingredient, and there is more than one way to build it.

Kelly, Humphreys & Ferri 2020, and related work. Sources

Three ways to start this week

  1. Go to one thing. A meeting, a class, a club session. Once. You're collecting information.
  2. Go back to the same thing. Community is made of repeat visits. By the third time, people know your name.
  3. Bring someone. Start a Crew Pact and send the link to one mate. Two people doing a thing is already a crew.
