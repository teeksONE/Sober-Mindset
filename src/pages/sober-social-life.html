What to say

You'll rehearse a speech. You'll need about four words. Most people ask once, out of politeness, and move on.

  • "I'm good, thanks."
  • "Not drinking tonight." (No reason required.)
  • "I'm driving."
  • "I've got training in the morning."
  • "I don't drink." (The one that ends the conversation for good.)

If someone keeps pushing after a clear no, that's about their drinking, not yours.

How to do a party

  1. Get a drink in your hand straight away. Soda and lime, a zero beer if those don't bother you, anything. A full hand stops the offers.
  2. Have your own way home. Never be stuck waiting for someone else's lift.
  3. Arrive late-ish, leave early-ish. The best two hours of any party are sober-friendly. After midnight it's the same conversation on a loop.
  4. Bring an ally or have one on the phone.
  5. Leave when it stops being fun. No goodbye tour needed.

In the first few months it is also completely fine to skip things. The party will happen again. Your early sobriety is the fragile bit.

Friends

Some friendships turn out to have been made of alcohol. Those fade, and it hurts more than you expect. Others get better, because you're actually there for them. Tell the ones who matter and suggest things that aren't the pub: a surf, a feed, a hike, a game. The ones who say yes are your friends.

The fastest way to make new ones is to show up at the same activity every week. Familiar faces turn into mates without anybody having to try. Find your crew has places to start.

Dating

"Let's get a drink" is a habit, not a law. Coffee, a walk, climbing, a market, a swim. Daytime dates are cheaper, you find out more about the person, and you remember the whole thing. Mention you don't drink early and plainly. Someone who has a problem with it has saved you several weeks.

You're less unusual than you think

Young adults across Australia, the UK and the US are drinking less than the generation before them, and more are not drinking at all. Zero-alcohol options are on most menus. Nobody's staring. Mostly, nobody's noticed.
