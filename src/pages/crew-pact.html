How it works

  1. Name the pact and pick a length.
  2. Send the link to one mate or a group chat. Anyone who opens it can join the same pact.
  3. Tick each day you keep it. Share your progress whenever you want a nudge or want to give one.

There are no accounts and no server. The pact travels in the link, and each person's ticks are stored on their own device. You see each other's progress when you share it.

Pacts that work

  • Small enough to keep on your worst day
  • Something you do, not only something you don't: "walk every morning" beats "don't drink" on its own
  • With one person you'd be embarrassed to let down
