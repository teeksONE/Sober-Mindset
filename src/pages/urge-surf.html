How it works

Urge surfing is a technique from relapse-prevention research. You don't fight the craving and you don't obey it. You watch it rise and fall like a wave. Then, because sitting still isn't for everyone, the tool gives you something with some intensity in it to do next.

The full explanation, with the evidence, is on the cravings page.

If it's more than a craving

If you're thinking about hurting yourself, or you can't keep yourself safe tonight, skip the timer. Go to Help now.
