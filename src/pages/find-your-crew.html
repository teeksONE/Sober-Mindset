Short, hard group workouts with a coach and the same faces every session. You'll find a lot of people who've got sober in these gyms. The training is built to leave you flooded with endorphins, which suits a brain that's used to chasing a hit.

A warning about clubs

Not every sports club is a safe place to be sober. Some run on post-game beers and end-of-season trips, and the research shows it.

A review of 17 long-term studies found that young people who played sport were less likely to use illicit drugs but, in most studies, more likely to drink. The sport isn't the problem. The culture around it can be.

Kwan et al., Addictive Behaviors, 2014. Sources

So choose with your eyes open. Good signs: early-morning sessions, a coach who takes training seriously, a sport where being hungover is dangerous, and members who talk about the next session and not the last night out.
