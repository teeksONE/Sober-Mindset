You're here if

  • You've got a few months and the novelty has worn off
  • You're sleeping badly, eating rubbish, or both
  • Feelings you haven't had since you were a teenager are turning up unannounced
  • You're wondering what sober people actually do on a Saturday night

The job of this stage

Rebuild the machine, then start filling the hours with things that are yours. The first page explains what your brain is doing on its own schedule, which makes the flat days much easier to sit through. Then the body, then feelings, then the social life.

One habit for this stage

A thirty-second daily check-in. Hungry, angry, lonely, tired: most slips in this stage start with one of the four, hours before the craving shows up.
