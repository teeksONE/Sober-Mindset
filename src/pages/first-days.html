You're here if

  • You've decided to stop, or you're on day one right now
  • You've stopped before and it didn't stick, and you want to do it properly
  • You're scared of what withdrawal will be like

Read this first

If you drink heavily every day, stopping suddenly can be dangerous. Not uncomfortable. Dangerous. The first page below tells you who needs a doctor before they stop. It takes three minutes.

The job of this stage

Stay safe, stay fed, stay busy in the evenings, and tell one person. I'm not going to ask you to fix your life this week. I couldn't have, and neither can you.
