What a standard drink is

The questions count standard drinks, and most real drinks are bigger. In Australia one standard drink is 10 grams of alcohol. Roughly:

  • A pint of full-strength beer is about 2 standard drinks
  • A typical pub glass of wine (150 ml) is about 1.5
  • A bottle of wine is 7 to 8
  • A single nip of spirits (30 ml) is 1

Australia's national guidelines say healthy adults should have no more than 10 standard drinks a week and no more than 4 on any one day, and that people under 18 should not drink at all. The AUDIT-C is a three-question version of the World Health Organization's AUDIT screening test and has been validated in many countries.

The signs that matter more than the number

A score is a screen. It can't see your life. These can:

  • You set a limit before you go out and regularly blow through it
  • You've tried to cut down and it lasted days
  • You need more than you used to for the same effect
  • You drink to get through things: social nerves, sleep, a bad day
  • You've lost chunks of the night more than once
  • Mornings involve shakes, sweats or dread that a drink would fix
  • Someone who loves you is worried

One or two of those and it's worth a straight conversation with a GP. They've heard it all, and it's confidential. If mornings involve the shakes, read Stopping safely before you change anything.
