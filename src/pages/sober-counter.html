Your date and numbers are stored in this browser only. They are never sent anywhere.

Why count at all

Because early on, a day sober doesn't feel like an achievement. It feels like a day where nothing happened. The counter is there to tell you the truth: something did.

Old-timers will tell you the only day that matters is today, and they're right. Count anyway. On a rough night, a number you don't want to reset has kept a lot of people out of the bottle shop.

The milestone notes come from published research on what changes after stopping. How the brain heals has the longer version.
