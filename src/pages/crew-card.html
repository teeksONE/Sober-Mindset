Build your card

Three people I can call

Ask them first. "Can I put you down as someone I call if it gets hard?" Almost everyone says yes, and asking is half the benefit.

What happens in the days before it gets bad? Skipping meals, not sleeping, cancelling plans, "I've got this now"?
The things that have actually got you through before.
A place that isn't home and doesn't serve alcohol. The gym, the beach, a mate's couch, a meeting.
One sentence, in your own words. You'll read it at the top of the card.

Saved in this browser only. Names and numbers never leave your device.

Print a copy and keep it in your wallet or on the fridge. If your browser data is cleared or you lose your phone, the paper still has it.

Why write it down

Counsellors call this a relapse prevention plan. The idea is simple. The part of your brain that makes good decisions goes quiet during a strong craving, so you make the decisions ahead of time and leave yourself instructions.

Print it or save it as a PDF and keep it where you'll see it. Update it whenever you learn something new about yourself, especially after a slip.
