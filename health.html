Health

"Take care of your body. It's the only place you have to live."

— Jim Rohn

In early sobriety the body is the first thing you fix. It is the most underrated part of recovery — when the body is steady, the mind follows. Sleep, food, movement, breath. Dial these four in and you have quietly solved seventy percent of the day-to-day work of staying sober.

Why These Four, In This Order

If you can only fix one thing this week, fix sleep. It is the foundation under every other practice. A body that did not sleep cannot eat well, train well, or sit with hard feelings without breaking.

Once sleep is steady, layer in nutrition — three meals, real food, enough protein. Stable blood sugar takes the volume down on cravings and anxiety more than people give it credit for.

Then movement. Even a daily walk. The body that gets used during the day is the one that sleeps at night and eats when it should.

Meditation goes last because it is the hardest to start cold. But once the other three are in place, five minutes of sitting becomes possible, then powerful. The whole stack reinforces itself.

This Week

Pick one. Start tomorrow.

Don't try to fix all four at once — you'll quit by Wednesday. Pick the one that is the most broken right now and put your whole attention there for seven days. The next one comes after.
